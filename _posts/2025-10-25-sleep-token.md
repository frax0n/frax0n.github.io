# Sleep Token : Vessel's Playground

![Alt text](/assets/img/SleepToken/st.png)

Exploring the lyrical and musical masterpiece that is Sleep Token.
Just wanted to explore some of the beautiful songs produced by this thankfully not so underrated now **Sleep Token**.

Surprisingly or not so suprisingly, I have been obsessed with this particular band as they blend different genres so seamlessly.They can pivot from pop into rap into a metal breakdown followed by a metalcore scream.Every song is pleasant surprise as I don't know what Vessel and his boys have in store for me.

## Let's explore some of their albums:
## Even in Arcadia -
![Alt text](/assets/img/SleepToken/EvenInAracadia.png)
Latest album released by Sleep Token at the moment with the most polished sound. They seemed have found their signature structure their sounds. Heavy metal with melancholic beginning.
## Dangerous
Tis the first song my monkey brain got to experience while shuffling through my recommendations based on a frankly extremely stupid playlist.

This song starts of as a deep meloncholic song which shifts into hip hop at the 1:30 with lyrics to match.

The dark lyrics while conveying the deep emotions are an absolute pleasure to listen with absolute banger of production to match.

The song then dives into a heavy metal breakdown, the chorus roaring at full power. The bridge pares things down to Vessel’s voice and gentle guitar strums, before everything erupts into a heavy-metal finish.

Well, this is going to be a pattern with these guys.


## Caramel

This song definitely has an angelic feel in the beginning with the mellow xylophone sampling with somber bass heavy synth which kicks in after first verse.

The bass heavy synth heavy actually holds the rap verse with the twinkly sound. It'd sound surprisingly hollow without it.

The lyrics talk about the expectations that come with being a celebrity and the people who want to see you fall from grace.
